I'm a Full-Stack Engineer working across web, mobile, backend systems
and AI. I enjoy moving between layers of a system : from product
interfaces and APIs to data, infrastructure and the engineering
decisions underneath them.

My work has ranged from production fullstack products, mobile applications and payment
systems to real-time platforms and RAG-based AI applications. I tend
to gravitate toward problems involving reliability, system design,
data flow and the parts of software that become interesting when
scale or failure enters the picture.

At **DigitalSherpa.AI**, I work across mobile, frontend, backend,
databases and applied AI, building and maintaining production
applications for Android and iOS. My work includes new product
development as well as taking ownership of existing systems,
platform-specific issues, native integrations and major dependency
upgrades.

Outside of work, I build systems such as **FocusFlow**, **Doom Tracker**, **PayCore**, **TubeScale** and a **RAG-powered** AI assistant for my portfolio, alongside other software projects.
